# AI assistance disclosure

OpenAI ChatGPT assisted with manuscript restructuring, English-language
refinement, document formatting, and software-release documentation. The human
authors remain responsible for the analyses, code, citations, interpretation,
and submitted text. Suggestions were checked against the archived result
tables, source code, cited sources, and GigaByte instructions.

AI tools were not listed as authors and did not autonomously determine the
reported empirical results. If the journal requests conversation-level output,
the corresponding author should export the relevant records after removing
unrelated personal information.
